# 100 model research priorities for Germany, Austria and Europe

Reviewed **2026-09-26**. [Machine-readable selection](models-100.json).

This is a concrete research queue, **not an exact pan-European Top 100** and not a claim that orVIN can decode all 100 models. Each record is a nameplate target; generations, model years, markets, WMI and variant boundaries still need decoding evidence.

## Selection

The queue starts with the 50 models in JATO’s Europe-28 full-year 2024 chart, adds the named models in Austria’s 2025 Top 50 that are absent, then fills the remaining places from Germany’s full-year 2025 registration totals. The JSON preserves original labels and exact source locators. `priority` is a research order, not a combined sales rank.

The resulting stages contain **50 European selections, 23 additional Austrian selections and 27 additional German selections**. Two Austrian “BUS” rows and the broad German MINI row cannot be allocated to a specific model and are recorded as exclusions.

German selection uses **column E, January–December 2025**, not December-only column D. Counts for combined rows are not apportioned: ID.4 and ID.5 are two targets supported by one source row. Original classification differences, including CUPRA models filed under SEAT and historical Mercedes model groups, remain visible.

These observations concern new registrations. They do not measure the whole installed fleet, prove a historical generation is popular, or give VIN decoding rules. Older Golf generations remain relevant research within the Golf target, but their popularity and rule applicability are not inferred from 2025 sales.

## Using the worklist

`models` contains 100 unique IDs, normalized make/model labels, a selection stage and source evidence. Every evidence entry points to a source record and a chart row, PDF table row or workbook cell range. German registration values belong to the original source row; a `count_scope` warning prevents treating a combined total as a single-model count.

Each target also has an explicit `astra_label_query` (make equality, case-insensitive type regex and optional exclusions), a count of distinct matching approval IDs, and up to three original type/template examples. These queries ran against the pinned ASTRA runtime projection. Its source/version and SHA-256 are recorded in the JSON. Counts include all source years and can overlap across targets. They are **text-query rows, not a count of decodable models or individually identified vehicles**. Zero means only that this exact query found no projected rows. A different label, an excluded malformed template or a recent model can explain a zero.

Queries evaluate literal shipped fields without hidden trimming. Anchored expressions explicitly allow leading whitespace. Cross-family exclusions cover C3 Aircross/Picasso/Pluriel, Toyota Cross/Verso derivatives, and ambiguous MINI family labels. These exclusions improve precision but do not make the search exhaustive.

These names and queries are search and curation targets, not a verified alias dictionary. Resolve type-approval labels, manufacturer and brand distinctions, generations and mixed model groups before connecting records. MINI Cooper and BMW numeric series queries are particularly heuristic. Transporter-related T-series/Caravelle/Multivan labels are not silently merged. In particular, do not turn a marketing name or registration-row match into a VIN-to-engine, VIN-to-trim or model-year rule. ASTRA source attribution: **Swiss Federal Roads Office (ASTRA)**; original approval labels and templates remain factual examples of the scoped Swiss source.

The next useful step is to attach verified European type-approval/VIN evidence to these IDs, with source-row lineage and scope, then measure supported fields and ambiguity separately from selection coverage. No runtime files were changed by this research.
